Strategy scanner

Filter: a stock appears here only if (1) it is among the top-ranked Long watch or Short watch stocks in the Live monitor, (2) it has a high R Factor (1.5 or more), and (3) a setup is forming in it in the same direction (Long watch needs a Buy signal, Short watch a Sell signal). Only stocks that meet every condition are listed. Pullback: a bullish candle, then a small red candle that closes inside its range on lower volume, then a candle breaking above the red candle's high on higher volume (Buy; Sell is the mirror image). Range breakout: a tight range of recent candles, then a candle closing above the range high (Buy) or below the range low (Sell) on at least 1.5× the range's average volume. Entry = the level that must break. Screens only, not advice.
